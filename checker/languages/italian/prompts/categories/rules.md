# Rules of the categories of Italian

Rules that the descriptions of several categories share, in describing.md.

## Prompt: empty

Leave a text empty where the word has no such form. Where a form has two accepted spellings, give both, separated by a slash: `devo / debbo`, `vo / vado`, `tra / fra`.

## Prompt: listing

List every form the word takes as one of `forms`, each with `tags` that say which it is, in small letters and in Italian, as gender and number, or what it is used as: `maschile singolare`, `femminile plurale`, `oggetto diretto`, `oggetto indiretto`, `elisa` for an elided form. Give a spelling once for every use it serves.
