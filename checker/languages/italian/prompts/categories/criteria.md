# Recognising a category

How the decision model is told to recognise a word of each category of Italian. It does best with examples, of every form the words come in.

## Prompt: noun

A noun, in the singular or the plural, also after an elided article: cane, cani, casa, case, uomo, uomini, città, uova, amico in l'amico.

## Prompt: verb

A verb, in any form: infinitive (andare, essere, lavarsi), conjugated (vado, vai, andavo, andò, andrò, andrei, vada, andassi, sono, è, ha, hanno, fu), participle (andato, andata, stato, fatto), gerund (andando, facendo).

## Prompt: adjective

An adjective, in any gender and number, or in its superlative: bello, bella, belli, belle, grande, grandi, felice, blu, bellissimo, migliore.

## Prompt: adverb

An adverb, which never changes but for a few: oggi, domani, adesso, presto, sempre, spesso, qui, là, molto, bene, meglio, velocemente, forse.

## Prompt: pronoun

A pronoun, also joined to a verb: io, me, mi, tu, te, ti, lui, lo, gli, lei, la, le, ci, vi, loro, si, ne, chi, che, cosa, qualcuno, niente, questo, quello.

## Prompt: article

An article or determiner, also elided: il, lo, la, l', i, gli, le, un, uno, una, un', mio, tuo, questo, ogni, alcuni.

## Prompt: preposition

A preposition, also fused with an article: di, a, da, in, con, su, per, tra, fra, del, della, al, allo, dal, nel, nella, sul, sugli.

## Prompt: conjunction

A conjunction: e, ed, o, ma, perché, che, se, quando, mentre, anche, però, quindi, benché.

## Prompt: particle

A particle: non, sì, no, mica, pure, proprio, ecco.

## Prompt: numeral

A number written as a word: uno, due, tre, otto, venti, cento, mille, primo, terza.

## Prompt: interjection

A greeting or exclamation: ciao, salve, arrivederci, grazie, prego, ah, oh, boh, magari.
