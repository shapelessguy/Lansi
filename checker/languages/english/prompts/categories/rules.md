# Rules of the categories of English

Rules that the descriptions of several categories share, in describing.md.

## Prompt: empty

Leave a text empty where the word has no such form. Where a form has two accepted spellings, give both, separated by a slash: `learned / learnt`, `dreamed / dreamt`, `fishes / fish`.

## Prompt: listing

List every form the word takes as one of `forms`, each with `tags` that say which it is, in small letters and in English: `object`, `possessive`, `possessive determiner`, `reflexive`, `plural`. Give a spelling once for every use it serves.
