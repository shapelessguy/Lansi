# Log of a translation

What the log of a translation says, which is written without a model, on two lines.

Variables:
- `$selection`: what was selected
- `$sentence`: the sentence it was in, with the selection between [[ and ]]
- `$translation`: the translation

## Prompt: asked

Asked for "$selection" to be translated, in: "$sentence".

## Prompt: translated

Translated as: "$translation".
