# The lesson

How the lesson practised is told: its heading, then each point taught or reviewed on a line of its own. The logs of the exercises tell the points of the lesson in the same way.

Variables:
- `$title`: the title of the lesson, or of the point
- `$level`: the level of the lesson
- `$role`: whether the point is new, reviewed or previewed
- `$statement`: the rule of the point
- `$when`: when the rule applies

## Prompt: heading

Lesson: $title (level $level)

## Prompt: point

- $title ($role): $statement Applies when: $when.
