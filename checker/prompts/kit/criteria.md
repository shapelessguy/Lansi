# Rating criteria

How what the learner writes or says is rated, from 0 to 4, as the examiners of the Goethe-Institut rate it, by the level of the exam. Each is told on a line of its own, after its name.

## Prompt: aufgabe

Task fulfilment. 4: every point of the task dealt with, with the detail the level asks for, about as long as asked, in the register the reader calls for; 3: every point, one only thinly; 2: one point missing or two thin; 1: most points missing or not to be understood; 0: off the task, or almost nothing.

## Prompt: kohaerenz

Coherence. 4: one text, its sentences joined with the connectors of the level, with a fitting greeting and ending where the kind of text has them; 3: joined mostly with und and dann, or the greeting or the ending missing; 2: a list of sentences that still reads as one text; 1: hard to follow; 0: not to be followed.

## Prompt: wortschatz

Vocabulary. 4: the words of the level used rightly, with some range; 3: a few wrong or missing words that do not get in the way; 2: several, so that the reader must guess now and then, or a very narrow range; 1: so many that the meaning is unclear; 0: hardly any words of the language.

## Prompt: strukturen

Structures. 4: the grammar of the level right, with at most a few slips; 3: some mistakes that do not get in the way; 2: many mistakes, though most sentences can be understood; 1: mistakes in most sentences, hard to understand; 0: no sentence is formed.

## Prompt: aussprache

Pronunciation. 4: easy to understand, with the sounds and the stress of the language; 3: an accent that does not get in the way; 2: the listener must make an effort; 1: hard to understand; 0: not to be understood.
