# Sentences of each level

What the sentences a learner reads and writes are like at each level, which level.md tells.

## Prompt: A1

very short, simple main clauses of about 4 to 8 words

## Prompt: A2

short sentences of about 6 to 12 words: main clauses, joined at most by simple conjunctions such as and, but or because

## Prompt: B1

sentences of about 8 to 15 words, with a subordinate clause where one fits

## Prompt: B2

sentences of about 10 to 20 words, with subordinate and relative clauses

## Prompt: C1

sentences of natural length, up to about 25 words, with complex structures

## Prompt: C2

sentences as a native speaker writes them, of any length and structure
