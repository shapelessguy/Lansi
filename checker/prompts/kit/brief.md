# The tutor's brief

Told to an exercise that the tutor offered in a chat with the learner, on lines of its own: what the tutor wants it to make. The tutor knows the learner and what they agreed to work on, and the exercise does not, so the brief rules over everything else it is told.

Variables:
- `$brief`: what the tutor wrote of what the exercise is to make

## Prompt

The learner's tutor, who knows them and agreed with them on what to practise now, wrote this brief of what you are to make. It comes before everything else you are told here: make exactly what it asks, item by item where it lists the items, and an item it gives word for word as it is, whole, however long it is, even a text of several sentences, and where the lesson, the topics, the words or anything else above go another way, follow the brief. Keep to the form your reply must have, and to the learner's level unless the brief asks for another. What you are told after the brief of what the learner had before, or of what was made already, is there so that you do not repeat yourself: it never keeps you from an item the brief gives.
