# The exercise of a log

What is said of the exercise a log is written of, one to a line in this order; the options as they were chosen come after `instances`, and the points of the lesson after `lesson`, as `point` of kit/lesson.md.

Variables:
- `$title`: the title of the exercise, or of the lesson
- `$description`: what the exercise is
- `$level`: the learner's level
- `$count`: how many instances there were to be
- `$topics`: the topics, joined by ;

## Prompt: heading

$title: $description

## Prompt: level

Level: $level

## Prompt: instances

Instances to do: $count

## Prompt: topics

Topics: $topics

## Prompt: lesson

Lesson: $title
