# Is the word of a category?

What the decision model is asked about a word, for each category and for each of not_stored.md, all at once.

Variables:
- `$language`: the language the learner is learning, such as German
- `$criterion`: how a word of the category is recognised, from categories/criteria.md

## Prompt

Is the $language `word`, as it is used in the `sentence` if one is given, this: $criterion
