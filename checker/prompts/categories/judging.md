# Judging a word of a category

What a language model is told about a word of any category when it judges whether the word is new, in a language that has no rules of its own in languages/: which is the dictionary form, and what is and is not the same word.

## Prompt

The dictionary form is the one a dictionary of the language lists the word under. The same word: every inflected form of it, and the same spelling with another meaning. Words of their own: words made from it with a prefix or a suffix, and compounds.
