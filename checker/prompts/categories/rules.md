# Rules of the categories

Rules that the descriptions of several categories share, in describing.md and in the descriptions of the generic categories.

## Prompt: meanings

`meanings` are short English translations, one for each distinct meaning of the word, the most common first.

## Prompt: empty

Leave a text empty where the word has no such form. Where a form has two accepted spellings, give both, separated by a slash: `Produkts / Produktes`, `geh / gehe`, `gern / gerne`.

## Prompt: listing

List every form the word is declined into as one of `forms`, each with `tags` that say which it is, in small letters, as case, then gender or `plural`: `dativ maskulin`, `akkusativ plural`. Give a spelling once for every case and gender it serves.
