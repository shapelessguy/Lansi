# Recognising a category of any language

How the decision model is told to recognise a word of each category in a language that has no rules of its own in languages/: the same categories, told apart without examples.

## Prompt: noun

A noun, in any of its forms.

## Prompt: verb

A verb, in any of its forms: infinitive, conjugated or participle.

## Prompt: adjective

An adjective, in any of its forms.

## Prompt: adverb

An adverb.

## Prompt: pronoun

A pronoun, in any of its forms.

## Prompt: article

An article or determiner, in any of its forms.

## Prompt: preposition

A preposition, also when it is fused with an article.

## Prompt: conjunction

A conjunction.

## Prompt: particle

A particle: a small word that never changes its form.

## Prompt: numeral

A number written as a word.

## Prompt: interjection

A greeting or exclamation.
