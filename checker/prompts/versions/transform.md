# Transforming a book

What a version of a book is written anew with, a part of the book at a time: the prompts the learner can start from, and change, when they make a transformed version. Each variant is one the learner is offered by its name. Every part of the book that is independent of the others, such as a story of a book of short stories, is transformed on its own.

They are not filled in by the server: they have the keywords of the prompts the learner writes, such as `{{language}}` and `{{level}}`, which the app fills in as it does in every other prompt of theirs.

## Prompt: translate

Translate the text into {{language}}. Keep its meaning, its tone and the way it is divided into paragraphs, and keep the names of people and places as they are. Write natural {{language}}, as a writer of {{language}} would have written the story, rather than a word-for-word translation.

## Prompt: level

Rewrite the text in {{language}} for a learner at level {{level}}. Keep the story, its events, its characters and the order of its paragraphs, but use the words and the grammar a learner at {{level}} knows: shorter sentences, common words, and tenses suited to the level. Keep each paragraph a paragraph of its own.
